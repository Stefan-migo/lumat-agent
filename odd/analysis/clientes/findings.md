## Shared-primitive roll-up

No clientes finding requires a NEW shared-primitive capability. The cross-section capabilities this
section needs already exist: `Dialog` exposes `icon`/`description` (`web/src/components/primitives/Dialog.tsx:10-12`)
and `Input` exposes `icon`/`helper` (`web/src/components/primitives/Input.tsx:13,15`), both shipped with the
Usuarios pass. Rows `clientes-020` and `clientes-022` are therefore local wiring, not upstream work.

Row-selection and pagination findings (`clientes-005`, `clientes-006`) are page-composable: selection can be a
page-provided column/state, and pagination can slice rows before passing them to `DataTable`; neither requires
changing `web/src/components/shared/DataTable.tsx`.
