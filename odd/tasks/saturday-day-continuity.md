# Saturday availability and day-selection continuity

The day the bot just offered is not treated as the customer's selection, and a day question falls to a generic policy sentence.
Branch: `odd/saturday-day-continuity` · Runner: `scripts/test.sh` · Worktree: `../lumat-agent-odd-saturday-day-continuity`.

## Design decisions

- **D1** — `availability_reply` claims a turn when it carries an availability term **or** when the customer's words resolve to a `SlotWindow` and the turn asks (question punctuation or a question marker). No keyword-only fix: the acceptance sentence carries no availability term.
- **D2** — A named day with no eligible slots answers truthfully from a new configured text (`availability_preview_day_none`), never with the generic policy sentence and never with an invented slot. No-window preview with no slots keeps returning `None` (existing behavior).
- **D3** — `hours_reply` keeps answering genuine business-hours questions untouched.
- **D4** — `orchestrate` routes a statement that resolves to a day/franja (no explicit question, not a change request, not a quote, not social-only, intent not `postventa`/`valores`) to `capture`: that is the existing visit-request path, which stores `captured_data["availability"]` and acknowledges it. No new state, no parallel scheduler.
- **D5** — Booking keeps reading the preference through `booking._requested_window` → `resolve_window`; the customer overlap filter from PR #898 stays in force.
- **D6** — A prose acceptance sentence that resolves to a window is stored as the window label (`window_label`), so the existing acknowledgement names the day instead of interpolating the whole sentence. Guarded so the pinned acknowledgement texts stay intact.
